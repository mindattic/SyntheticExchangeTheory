# Experiment design

The empirical program for Synthetic Exchange Theory, kept separate from the dissertation, which remains theoretical (Ryan, 2026-11-02; this work was started at his request on 2026-11-05, SET-T46).

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `program.md` | Why a staged program replaces the single archived pilot; which claims are definitional and which are empirical premises; six studies, each with its premise, design, primary outcome, the result that would count against the premise, and its limits |
| `prereg-study-1.md` | A full pre-registration draft for Study 1 (terms of contribution and the quality of human input), the test of the premise Proposition 4 depends on |
| `prereg-study-2.md` to `prereg-study-5.md` | Pre-registration drafts for Studies 2 to 5 (dependence, correlated remediation, floor versus outside option, blind convention), in the same form |
| `power.py` | Sample sizes for Studies 1, 4 and 5, computed analytically and checked by simulation with a fixed seed: `python -I research/experiment-design/power.py`. Study 2 sets its seed count from a pilot, and Study 3 computes its sample size in its own draft |

The archived pilot (`archive/pilot-2026-11-02/`) is Study 6, the last stage. It is not to be registered if Study 1 finds input quality equivalent across terms or worse under fair terms; an inconclusive Study 1 does not close that gate (`program.md`, Study 6).

Nothing here is registered, funded, approved by an ethics committee, or run. Before registration: pilot the materials, obtain ethics approval, choose the panel and payment levels, and record every change with its reason.
